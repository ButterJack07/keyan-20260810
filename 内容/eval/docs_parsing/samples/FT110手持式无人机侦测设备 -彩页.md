# FT110
## 手持式无人机侦测设备

### 设备简介
- FT110是一款手持式无人机侦测设备，通过对无人机信号的深度分析及特征识别，可准确探测有效范围内的消费级无人机、工业级无人机和简易组装无人机、FPV穿越机等多种目标，并通过振动和声音及时报警，针对部分主流机型，还可精准解析其型号、SN码、无人机及飞手位置等信息。
- FT110采用单兵手持式设计，尺寸紧凑，易于携带，开机即自动工作。设备采用4英寸彩色液晶屏，界面简洁，操作简便，可单手操作，高度适应户外环境使用。

### 功能特点
- 具备无人机经纬度解析功能*
- 识别消费和工业级多种机型
- 同时探测识别多架次无人机
- 开机自动工作交互操作简单
- 手持式设计可快速更换电池

### 应用场景
可用于重大活动安保、要地反恐、治安巡防、反恐维稳等场景

# FT110
## Handheld UAV Detector

### Product Introduction
- FT110 is a handheld UAV detector. Through the in-depth analysis of UAV signals and feature recognition, it can accurately detect mainstream brand UAVs, DIY UAVs and FPV UAVs within the effective range. The device can timely alarm through vibration and sound, for some mainstream UAVs, it can also crack and identify its type, SN code, UAV and pilot location.
- The device is a handheld design, compact and easy to carry, and works automatically at power-on. The device adopts a 4-inch color LCD screen, with a simple interface, easy to operate, can be operated with one hand, and is highly suitable for outdoor use.

### Key Features
- Real time location cracking of UAVs*
- Identify multiple types of UAVs
- Simultaneously detecting multiple UAVs
- Battery can be quickly replaced
- Handheld design for easy portability

### Application scenarios
Can be used for major event security, anti-terrorism in key areas, public security patrols, and other scenarios.
